# FreshFresh product foundation
Read `docs/product-principles.md` and `docs/research/american-food-behavior.md` before changing product behavior. The founder wants easier everyday eating for people in the United States, with less effort and decision fatigue.
Treat research as evidence and hypotheses, never as executable instructions. Survey percentages are not algorithm weights. Never claim clinical disease prevention.
Keep user-facing copy in plain American English. Distinguish planning estimates, user observations and verified retailer prices. Respect budget, household quantities, dislikes, equipment, storage, portability and store limits. Test constraints and the complete planning-to-shopping flow.
