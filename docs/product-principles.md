# Product foundation

The founder's goal is easier everyday eating that supports looking after yourself. The supplied September 17, 2026 behavioral research is the continuing product reference, not clinical evidence or proof of product demand.

| Pain or research hypothesis | Product decision | Acceptance condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enjoyment and familiar foods matter | Familiar meals, dislikes, swaps | Excluded ingredients never enter a meal |
| Checkout spend differs from serving cost | Whole packages and ingredient reuse | Round package counts after household scaling and pantry subtraction |
| Time and tools limit execution | No-cook assembly and equipment filters | No heating recipe when cooking is disabled |
| Packed lunches have storage constraints | Separate refrigeration at home and cold storage away | Exclude meals that cannot be stored safely |
| Extra markets take effort | Cap allowed stores | All items belong to the selected store set |
| Too many decisions add work | Short setup and a small curated meal collection | Full guest flow without registration |
| Plans fail on busy days | Compatible quick swaps | Swaps preserve constraints and the checkout budget |
| Unused food wastes money | Pantry quantities | A partial package offsets only that quantity |
| Nutrition can be confusing | Descriptive food-group coverage | No invented nutrient scores or adequacy claims |
| Prices change in store | Dated, editable package prices | Totals update and flag budget overruns |

## Boundaries
Survey percentages come from different populations and are not algorithm weights. Behavioral segments and retention hypotheses require user testing. The curated catalog uses clearly labeled planning estimates, not live stock or official retailer prices. User-entered prices belong to a particular package and selected store. This release does not aggregate community reports for generic ingredients because an exact SKU/location match is unavailable.

A meal plan covers only the requested meals. Food-group presence is descriptive, not nutrient sufficiency. Ingredient exclusions cannot verify packaging or manufacturing cross-contact.

Portable perishables require cold storage. USDA reference: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/keeping-bag-lunches-safe

## Product validation
Observe buying, preparing and eating with 10–15 varied users as exploratory research. Measure completed meals, accepted swaps, actual checkout versus estimate, effort, leftovers and willingness to repeat. Do not call projected serving costs verified savings.
